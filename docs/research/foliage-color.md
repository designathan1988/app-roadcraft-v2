# Foliage color-space correction

The [three.js color management guide](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/color-management.html) and [Color API](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Color.html) state that hexadecimal inputs are automatically converted from sRGB into the Linear-sRGB working space, including the values written to vertex-color attributes. The prop generator, flower tints and relative road-class tints applied `convertSRGBToLinear()` again. The broadleaf source `0x5c8c33` therefore entered the GPU with green `0.0559` rather than `0.2623`, making trees and hedges almost black.

The extra conversion was removed, and foliage hex palettes were retuned by eye in the game's fixed-camera scene because their old values had been chosen around the incorrect conversion. [GPU Gems 3's SpeedTree chapter](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-i-geometry/chapter-4-next-generation-speedtree-rendering) and [Crysis vegetation shading chapter](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-iii-rendering/chapter-16-vegetation-procedural-animation-and-shading-crysis) both emphasize that leaf lighting and color variation matter to a tree's perceived volume; this correction preserves the existing geometry, instancing and LOD while restoring the intended color space.
